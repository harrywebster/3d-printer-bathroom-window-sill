# v2 — superseded by v3, do not use this 3MF

Added after shipping. The geometry here is fine, and identical to v3. The 3MF
is not: Bambu Studio treats it as a plain 3MF, ignores its plates and puts all
five parts on plate 1, outside the bed ("Nothing to be sliced"). v3 ships the
same geometry as a proper Bambu Studio project, one part per plate. Print v3.
